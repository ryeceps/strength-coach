USE THE PLAN

How to use these plans

Open a plan and choose a week. The exercise notes tell you what each lift is doing. The body map shows planned direct and supporting work; it does not measure muscle activation or recovery.

If the schedule flags hard work close together, look at your performance, soreness, and sleep before changing the plan. Download the Excel log and record loads, reps, and notes each session. An adjustment made in the HTML does not change the downloaded workbook.

These plans are educational examples, not medical advice. Get clearance from a qualified clinician before starting a full training plan.
