STRONGMAN · 12 WEEKS · 4 DAYS

RPE pyramid

Log, deadlift, and squat top sets move from sixes to fives to triples. Backoff work follows the day's effort. Upper back and pressing work fill the week without turning every session into an event day.

STRONGMAN · 12 WEEKS · 4 DAYS

Four-day rebuild

A steady return to four training days. The main lifts and event work have their own places in the week. Build useful work first, then push intensity when the training supports it.

BODYBUILDING · 4 WEEKS · 4 DAYS

Priority muscle block

Train hamstrings and side delts twice a week while keeping the rest of the body moving. Three weeks build effort. The fourth backs off so the next block starts fresh.

USE THE PLAN

Log it. Review it. Adjust it.

Open a plan and choose a week. The exercise notes tell you what each lift is doing. The body map shows planned direct and supporting work; it does not measure muscle activation or recovery.

If the schedule flags hard work close together, look at your performance, soreness, and sleep before changing the plan. Download the Excel log and record loads, reps, and notes each session. An adjustment made in the HTML does not change the downloaded workbook.

These plans are educational examples, not medical advice. Get clearance from a qualified clinician before starting a full training plan.
