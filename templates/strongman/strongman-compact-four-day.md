# Strongman Compact Four Day

## Gist
- Four-day strongman template for athletes who want 45-60 minute sessions.
- Uses three training slots per day: focused lift or event, support superset, accessory superset.
- Best for spreading a 3-day strongman plan over more days without bloating each workout.

## Best Use
- 4 day weeks.
- 8 to 12 week strongman base, SPP, or contest-prep blocks.
- Athletes who need more event exposure but have short session windows.
- Programs built around log press, deadlift, front squat, farmer carries, yoke, stones, sandbag, or medley work.

## Phase Bias
- Primary phase: GPP into SPP, or SPP into Peak depending on event specificity.
- Volume or rebuild phases can hold RPE steady.
- Strength build phases should usually ramp RPE or load week to week.

## Weekly Shape
- Day 1: overhead or log focus.
- Day 2: deadlift or hinge focus.
- Day 3: carry, load, yoke, stones, or medley focus.
- Day 4: squat, secondary press, or lagging event focus.

## Session Shape
- Slot A: focused lift or event, 18-25 minutes.
- Slot B: support lift supersetted with antagonistic or low-interference counterpattern work, 12-18 minutes.
- Slot C: accessory superset, 8-12 minutes.

## Progression Pattern
- Weeks 1-4 rebuild or volume: fixed submaximal work around `RPE 6-7`.
- Weeks 5-8 strength build: ramp from about `RPE 7` to `RPE 8`, then hold intensity with clean work or slightly lower volume.
- Weeks 9-11 heavy triples or event specificity: top triples or event runs ramping `RPE 8`, `RPE 8.5`, `RPE 9`.
- Week 12 deload or controlled test: default to `RPE 6-7`; optionally test one focused lift or event only if the athlete is recovering well.

## What To Steal
- Short-session structure that still preserves strongman specificity.
- Carries and loading get a focused day instead of being squeezed after deadlifts.
- Pressing support is paired with upper-back antagonistic work.
- Lower-body and carry support uses trunk, upper-back, or single-joint pairings that do not compromise bracing.
- Accessories are the first cut when sessions exceed the time cap.

## Blend Notes
- Start from the athlete's event list. If there is no contest, use broad strongman staples: log, deadlift, front squat, farmer carry, loading, upper back, trunk, and grip.
- Keep the exact implement first when contest specificity matters.
- If recovery is limited, reduce Slot C rounds before changing Slot A.
- For a 3-day source program, move the most fatiguing carry, load, yoke, or medley element into Day 3 before adding new exercises.
